# Community Code of Conduct

TraceForge welcomes contributors with different experience levels. Discussions should be respectful, constructive and focused on improving the tool.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- Be courteous and assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give specific, actionable feedback rather than personal attacks.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats and disclosure of private information.
- Respect security disclosure procedures and never post sensitive PCAPs or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate comments and restrict participation when needed. To report concerning behavior, contact the maintainer privately through an available verified GitHub-profile contact route. Do not expose personal information in public discussions.

This policy applies to TraceForge's official repository issues, pull requests, and associated project discussions.
